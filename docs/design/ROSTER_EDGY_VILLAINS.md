# GACHARGE! (ガチャージ): Edgy Roster & Villain Redesign Pass

**Scope:** villain side (Emperor Junkrown, 4 lieutenants, 10 Rust Legion enemies with purified forms) plus 20 edgy, dark and cool-rival Kachibots, including 3 alternate concepts for the rival Kyo Aonami's partner **AOGIRI**. The document ends with 4 ready-to-paste Gemini prompts.

**Status:** these are working concepts for the owner to pick from. Names are unchecked for trademarks (see `docs/NAMES.md`).

## 0. Ground rules used for every design

**Tone**
- Edgy means sharp silhouettes, dark values, glowing eyes, attitude and rivalry.
- It never means gore, exposed insides, blood, real weapons of harm or horror jump-scares.
- Every design must still read as a **plastic toy**: visible seams, peg joints, screw holes, sprue nubs, paint-app edges, stickers, wind-up keys and battery doors.
- Rust Legion corruption is **purple rust crust + acid-green crack glow**, painted on like a decal. It is never organic rot.

**Villain code** (from `research_notes/.../toy_mecha_anime_design_language.md` §6)
- Mono-eyes, slits or hidden faces.
- Horns and a "broken V" instead of hero fins.
- Asymmetric arms on symmetric legs.
- Secondary-color palettes: purple + acid green for the Rust Legion; purple + orange for the comic lieutenants; violet + ice blue for Junkrown's "cold" phases.
- Villain bodies sit in a darker value band than heroes.

**Purification code**
- A villain's purified form keeps its **mesh and silhouette**. The palette, eye shape and one "corruption part" change:
  - rust crust → clean candy-plastic;
  - mono-eye slit → two round eyes, or a friendly visor;
  - one cracked or jagged part → a restored or rounded part.
- This way one model gives two collectibles.

**Avoid-list check** (applied to every entry)
- No V-fin with a chest-vent tricolor (Gundam).
- No vehicle-to-robot panel shuffling (Transformers).
- No medal-in-chest insect/animal "medarot" bodies (Medabots).
- No realistic mechanical-animal skeletons with wind-up bases (Zoids).
- No digital-monster evolution lines with creature anatomy (Digimon).
- No animatronic mascot suits, endoskeleton-in-costume or pizza-restaurant horror (FNaF). Our haunted toys are **folk-toy yokai and playroom objects**, never costumed animatronics.

**Size classes and cost** (cost is Charge Points; squad cap **30**, as in the mockup brief)

| Class | Height vs Standard | Cost range | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mini | 0.5–0.7x | 1–3 | Swarmers, pets, support |
| Standard | 1x (~10 cm toy) | 3–6 | Core roster |
| Heavy | 1.3–1.6x | 6–8 | Tanks, bruisers |
| Giant | 2–3x | 9–12 | Lieutenant battle forms, elites |
| Colossal | 4x+ | Boss only | Junkrown and kaiju raids |

**Faction palette anchors**

|---|---|---|
| Rust Purple | `#5B2A86` | Main villain color |
| Deep Rust | `#3A1A4F` | Shadow value |

## 1. Emperor Junkrown: 3 alternative concepts

Every concept follows the lore brief: a tyrant built from broken, fused toys, with the final form **GACHAOS**. Each has a throne form (for cutscenes), a battle form and an arena-scale boss gimmick.

### Concept J-A: **JUNKROWN, the Toybox Tyrant** (玩具箱の暴君)
- **Series:** Rust Legion, Emperor
- **Size class:** Colossal (boss only, not collectible). The purified form, **Kingcap**, is Standard, cost 6.
- **Role:** multi-phase raid boss
- **Silhouette hook:** a giant **toy chest** throne with a lid-crown, on four mismatched legs:
  - a baby-doll leg;
  - a robot tread;
  - a plush bear paw;
  - a chess rook.
- **Visual design**
  - The "emperor" is a small cracked **tin wind-up king** fused into the chest's front like a figurehead.
  - A giant brass wind-up key turns slowly in his back.
  - The chest lid is a crown of broken capsule halves, jagged like teeth, with acid-green cracks running between the planks.
  - A cape of torn gift-wrap and ribbon hangs from the lid hinges.
  - One mono-eye is a cracked marble in the tin king's face.
  - Stickers peel off everywhere: "PROPERTY OF ___" and "DO NOT OPEN".
- **Palette:** `#3A1A4F` chest wood, `#5B2A86` lacquer, `#C9A227` tarnished brass key, `#9BFF2E` cracks, `#C4561B` rust flakes, `#E8D9C0` tin-king face
- **Battle form, "Lid-Maw Rampage":** the chest stands up on its four legs. The lid becomes a huge chomping jaw, and the tin king rises on a spring neck to aim.
- **Moveset**
  - *Shot:* **Marble Gatling**, a spray of cracked marbles from the lid.
  - *Melee:* **Lid Crunch**, a lunging chomp.
  - *Special:* **Lost & Found**, a vacuum that pulls arena props (blocks, crayons) into the chest, then spits them back as homing junk.
  - *Super:* **Forgotten Parade**, which releases a stream of Rustlings as a conga-line wave.
- **Boss gimmick, "Wind-Down":**
  - The brass key's rotation is his power timer.
  - Every 30 seconds he "winds down" and slumps, and the key sits exposed on his back. That is the weak point.
  - Hitting the key while it spins makes him overwind and stagger.
  - Phase 2 is **GACHAOS**: the chest cracks open and a capsule-globe core floats out.
- **Why unique:** a boss that is a *container of forgotten toys*. The theme (abandonment) is the mechanic (he swallows your props and allies).

### Concept J-B: **JUNKROWN, the Broken Crank** (壊れたハンドル)
- **Series:** Rust Legion, Emperor
- **Size class:** Colossal (boss only). The purified form, **Lucky Crank**, is Heavy, cost 7.
- **Role:** gacha-machine tyrant and summoner
- **Silhouette hook:** a towering **corrupted capsule vending machine** with a globe head. Inside the globe dozens of cracked capsules tumble, each with a glowing eye, and they all look at you at once.
- **Visual design**
  - The body is the machine cabinet, dented, with a coin slot for a mouth that grinds.
  - The right arm is a giant chrome crank handle (club). The left arm is a chute that dispenses capsules.
  - He wears a crown of bent price-tag hooks and a sash of "OUT OF ORDER" tape.
  - Legs are stacked capsule-machine bases, so he walks slowly and stomps.
  - The display panel is a cracked LED ticker that scrolls "¥¥¥ JACKPOT ¥¥¥" in acid green.
- **Palette:** `#2B2140` cabinet, `#7B6CFF` cold violet chrome, `#A9E4FF` ice-blue globe glass, `#9BFF2E` capsule eyes, `#E23D6F` magenta LED, `#4A4E57` gunmetal
- **Battle form, "GACHAOS SPIN":** the globe detaches and hovers like a crowned moon, and the cabinet body becomes a walking turret.
- **Moveset**
  - *Shot:* **Capsule Barrage**, lobbed capsules that burst into mini Rustlings.
  - *Melee:* **Crank Crusher**, a 360° handle sweep.
  - *Special:* **Rigged Roll**, a 3-reel slot above his head that picks his next attack combo.
  - *Super:* **JACKPOT OF RUIN**, a coin avalanche in an arena-wide wave with safe gaps.
- **Boss gimmick, "Hit the Reels":**
  - Each phase starts with a 3-reel slot spin.
  - Players can **shoot the reels** to change the outcome. Three stars cancel his attack; three skulls-in-capsules summon elites.
  - Final phase: players crank his own handle backwards (a QTE on the wrist Kachi Dial) to "un-pull" him.
- **Why unique:** the villain is a dark mirror of the very machine that gives the heroes life. The fight is a fight against bad luck.

### Concept J-C: **JUNKROWN, Scrapheap Shogun** (廃品将軍)
- **Series:** Rust Legion, Emperor
- **Size class:** Colossal (an arena you partly fight *on*). The purified form, **Prize Pal**, is Mini, cost 2.
- **Role:** climbable arena boss
- **Silhouette hook:** a colossal armored emperor made of fused junk.
  - Two free-floating **claw-machine claws** serve as hands.
  - A cape of torn trading cards flows behind him.
  - He has a split face: half a cracked porcelain doll mask, half a flickering LED screen.
- **Visual design**
  - The armor plates are toy-car bodies, building-block bricks and cereal-box cardboard, all fused and "rusted" purple.
  - The kabuto-like crest is a snapped hero-toy V-fin with one side broken off. It is the corrupted-hero symbol.
  - A glowing capsule reactor sits in the chest, with a tiny silhouette curled up inside it.
  - The claw hands have three prongs and dangling cables, and hover independently.
  - Parts of the body are walkable ramps, like a moving fortress.
- **Palette:** `#1E1A26` black-iron, `#5B2A86` purple rust, `#E2DDD3` porcelain, `#9BFF2E` crack glow, `#FF9A3C` LED amber, `#C9A227` tarnished gold trim
- **Battle form, "Shogun Unbound":** the cape unfurls into thousands of card-bats. The mask half slides off, and the LED face shows a single enormous eye.
- **Moveset**
  - *Shot:* **Card Storm**, homing trading-card blades (paper, rounded corners).
  - *Melee:* **Claw Slam**, where a claw drops like a UFO-catcher onto a marked circle.
  - *Special:* **Grab & Drop**, where a claw grabs a Kachibot and carries it toward the "prize chute" pit, and allies must shoot the claw to free it.
  - *Super:* **Scrapfall**, where the arena ceiling of junk collapses along a telegraphed grid.
- **Boss gimmick, "Climb the Tyrant":**
  - Destroy both claws, then ride the arm ramps up to the chest capsule.
  - Final reveal: the core holds a **tiny, sad, original prize toy**, the first toy ever left behind.
  - Purifying him produces **Prize Pal**, an emotional capstone.
- **Why unique:** the only boss in the roster you traverse. The UFO-catcher claw is instantly toy-culture and never used as a boss motif in the reference franchises.

## 2. The four lieutenants: 2 concepts each

### 2.1 Jester Jinx (clown marionette)

#### Concept JJ-A: **JESTER JINX, Puppet-Crown** (操り道化)
- **Series:** Rust Legion, Lieutenant
- **Size class and cost:**
  - Standard in cutscenes; Giant in battle form (boss only).
  - Purified **Jinx Jolly** is Standard, cost 5.
- **Role:** controller/disabler boss
- **Silhouette hook:** a lanky wooden marionette hanging from a **floating cross-shaped control bar that is also his crown**. It reads as a figure dangling beneath a halo.
- **Visual design**
  - Wooden ball joints with visible pins; a diamond-pattern tabard in two clashing colors.
  - A porcelain-white face with a painted crescent grin. The eyes are two hollow holes with green pinpoint lights.
  - Strings glow acid green and look like thin light threads.
  - His two-pronged jester hat ends in bells that are tiny cracked capsules.
  - One hand holds a hand-puppet of himself that talks back.
- **Palette:** `#5B2A86` purple, `#FF7A1A` orange (comic menace pairing), `#F4EEE2` porcelain, `#9BFF2E` strings, `#1C1424` shadow
- **Battle form, "Cross-Bar Tyrant":** the control bar grows into a giant flying cross-shaped drone. The puppet body swings beneath it like a wrecking ball.
- **Moveset**
  - *Shot:* **Bell Toss**, bouncing bell bombs.
  - *Melee:* **Pendulum Kick**, a swinging body strike.
  - *Special:* **String Tag**, a thread that latches onto a Kachibot and **inverts its controls** for 5 s.
  - *Super:* **Curtain Call**, which pulls theater curtains shut over the arena for a darkness phase lit only by his eye-lights.
- **Boss gimmick, "Cut the Strings":** his 8 strings are targetable. Each string cut makes a limb go limp and changes his attack pattern. Cutting all 8 drops the body; then you attack the control-bar crown.
- **Why unique:** the fight is about the *puppet relationship*. You attack what controls him, not him.

#### Concept JJ-B: **JESTER JINX, Jack-in-the-Crate** (びっくり箱)
- **Series:** Rust Legion, Lieutenant
- **Size class and cost:** Standard, becoming a Giant battle form. Purified **Pop-Jinx** is Standard, cost 4.
- **Role:** trickster ambusher
- **Silhouette hook:** a **jack-in-the-box** with a tall accordion-spring neck. The crank on the box side is his "heart".
- **Visual design**
  - The box is a cube with harlequin diamonds and dented corners. The lid flaps are torn ribbons.
  - His head is a big smiling mask with **two faces** (grin and frown) that spin like a coin.
  - His arms are springy slinky coils ending in white gloves.
  - The crank turns on its own, and a warped music-box tune plays.
- **Palette:** `#3A1A4F` box, `#FF7A1A` diamonds, `#F2E23B` gloves, `#9BFF2E` crank glow, `#F4EEE2` mask
- **Battle form, "Pop Goes the Jinx":** 9 boxes appear in the arena and he can pop out of any of them.
- **Moveset**
  - *Shot:* **Confetti Cannon**, which blinds (screen confetti).
  - *Melee:* **Spring Jab**, a 3-hit slinky punch.
  - *Special:* **Box Shuffle**, which swaps the boxes like a shell game.
  - *Super:* **Grand Surprise**, where all 9 boxes burst at once and the fake heads explode into confetti.
- **Boss gimmick, "Whack-a-Jinx":**
  - The music-box tune is the tell: the box he'll pop from plays the tune slightly louder.
  - Crank the real box backwards and he gets stuck halfway out, vulnerable.
- **Why unique:** audio-cued boss fight; an arena-wide shell game.

## 3. Edgy / cool Kachibots: 20 toys

### Series: SHADOW NINJA (影忍シリーズ)
*Series trait: every toy has one "paper" material element (origami, washi or ink) so the line is not generic black-ninja.*

#### 1. **ORIKAGE** (折影, "folded shadow")
- **Series:** Shadow Ninja. **Size class:** Standard, cost 5. **Role:** assassin / stealth striker
- **Silhouette hook:** the whole body is **folded origami panels**. The head is a folded crane-head helm with a long pointed beak visor, and the shoulders are folded wing-flaps.
- **Visual design**
  - Crisp faceted planes with visible fold creases, like a paper model rendered in hard plastic.
  - Black washi paper with a silver-flake print.
  - Two thin red slit eyes under the beak.
  - It refolds between forms: crane, shuriken star, flat sheet for hiding.
- **Palette:** `#16161C` black washi, `#8E9AAF` silver fleck, `#E0283E` eye red, `#F4F1EA` inner-fold white
- **Moves**
  - *Shot:* **Paper Star**, folded throwing stars that arc.
  - *Melee:* **Crease Cut**, fast 4-hit.
  - *Special:* **Flat Fold**, which folds flat and slides under cover (invisible while still).
  - *Super:* **Thousand Cranes**, a swarm of paper cranes homing in.
- **Why unique:** a ninja defined by *folding* rather than a mask and scarf. It also works as a procedural faceted mesh.

#### 2. **SUMIGAKE** (墨駆け, "ink runner")
- **Series:** Shadow Ninja. **Size class:** Standard, cost 4. **Role:** zone-painter / trap setter
