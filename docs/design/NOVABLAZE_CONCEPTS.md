# NOVABLAZE — 15 Redesign Concepts

> **Problem:** the current mockup (`docs/mockups/mech-novablaze-sheet-A.jpg`) is a red/white SD robot with a gold V-crest, twin cyan eyes behind a faceplate, and a beam saber and blaster. It reads as SD Gundam at first glance.
> **Goal:** a hero capsule toy that is clearly *ours*: one you know from its silhouette alone, that works as a toy, that a kid would pick first, and that is tied to GACHARGE!'s ideas (gacha capsules, the Kira Spark coin, the Kachi Dial twist, falling stars, toy scale).

## Rules for every concept

- **No** V-fin, no twin-eye faceplate with a "mouth" grille, no beam saber, no Gundam-style panel lines or gold chevrons.
- **Avoid** these franchise tells: Transformers (a realistic car or truck split into limbs, a "faceplate + helmet ear-fins" head), Medabots (a medal inside the back, a beetle-horn head *plus* a head cannon), Mega Man (a blue helmet with ear discs and an arm buster), Digimon (a sleek anthro beast with a "-mon" feel), Power Rangers (a full visored helmet with a diamond visor).
- **Keep:** heroic and red-led (Hikaru's jacket is red), ~10 cm SD proportions (a head about 1/3 of the height), readable at thumbnail size, plus one tactile *toy gimmick* you can explain in a single sentence.
- **Tie-ins to hit:** the Kira coin (a falling-star coin), the twist of the Kachi Dial (a gacha handle), the capsule, "BLAZING NOVA BUSTER!!", Twin-Twist fusion, TRI-GIGANT (NOVABLAZE is the **core**; per the brief, the ninja toy becomes wings and a crest and the dino toy becomes chest armor and fists).
- **Watch the cast:** rival AOGIRI is a blue ninja-knight with a crescent crest and twin blades, so NOVA stays away from ninja, knight and crescent motifs. The Rust Legion grunt is a *rusty* wind-up tin robot (see the note on #03).

### Shared style line (paste into each prompt)
`Style: early-2000s TV anime cel look, clean black ink lines, 2-tone cel shading, bright saturated colors, crisp toy-design-sheet layout.`

### Shared sheet layout (already in every prompt below)
Front / side / back turnaround with a 10 cm scale bar, 3 action poses, a capsule-packaging panel, labelled callouts, the "GACHARGE!" logo with ガチャージ, and the model number. 4:3.

## 01 · KAPSULE CORE — *"The toy that IS the capsule."*

- **Body:** an egg-shaped torso made of the two capsule halves (a red top shell over a clear bottom shell). Stubby limbs pop out of the seam, like a toy that has "hatched".
- **Head:** a small clear dome, like a mini capsule, with a single glowing face inside: two star-shaped pupils and a printed smile. No faceplate, no fin, just a round red "cap" knob on top.
- **Gimmick:** the torso halves split along the seam and swing up onto the shoulders as big round pauldrons, which shows a spinning Kira coin reactor in the chest.
- **Weapon:** the **Kapsule Knuckle**, a fist that fires a whole capsule half like a boxing glove on a spring, and it comes back.
- **Evolution:** *Base* is a closed egg (a defense stance). *Kira Overdrive* has the halves up on the shoulders as wing-pauldrons, a flaming coin, and gold star rims on the capsule seams. *TRI-GIGANT core* is the open capsule becoming a giant chest cockpit that the other two toys lock around; the coin sits in the middle of the combined robot's chest.
- **Colors:** Capsule Red `#E3262E`, Clear Shell `#CFEFFF` (tinted), Seam White `#FAFAF5`, Kira Gold `#FFC21A`, Core Cyan `#3FE0FF`.
- **Silhouette:** a round egg on stubby legs, with two dome pauldrons that pop up like opened bubbles.
- **Why it's unique:** it is the brand itself. No other franchise's hero is the packaging. It is a perfect toy gimmick (pull it out of the capsule and the capsule is part of it), and it gives an instant unboxing moment.

## 02 · STARFALL FIVE — *"A falling star with fists."*

- **Body:** the whole robot is a five-point star. The head, two arms and two legs are the star's points (each ending in a chunky tapered block), and the chest is a big faceted star gem.
- **Head:** a pointed head-tip with **one large star-shaped eye** (a cyclops star) in the centre. Two small "sparkle" antennae sit off to one side, asymmetric like a twinkle.
- **Gimmick:** it tucks its arms and legs in and **spins as a giant star shuriken**. The toy has a peg on the chest back so it can be spun on a desk.
- **Weapon:** the **Twinkle Edge**: its star-point forearms flip out into five-point blades while it spins.
- **Evolution:** *Base* is a pastel-gold star with red trim. *Kira Overdrive* grows a long comet tail from the back (a flame plume made of clear orange plastic parts), and the star eye turns white-hot. *TRI-GIGANT core* is the star chest that becomes the combined robot's emblem-core; the other toys plug into its five points like a constellation.
- **Colors:** Nova Red `#E8322B`, Star Gold `#FFD23F`, Twinkle White `#FFFFFF`, Comet Orange `#FF7A1A`, Night Indigo `#2B2A6B` (joints).
- **Silhouette:** a chunky five-point star with fists: recognisable from the shape alone.
- **Why it's unique:** a star-shaped *body plan* (not just a star emblem) is rare in mecha. It ties straight to the Kira falling-star coin and has a built-in spin-toy gimmick.

## 03 · ZENMAI NOVA — *"Wind it up. Let it blaze."*

- **Body:** a 1950s tin-toy robot turned hero: a boxy barrel chest printed with lithographed stars and rivets, accordion-hose arms, clunky square feet on little wheels.
- **Head:** a box head with **round porthole eyes** (glass with star irises), a rectangular smile grille, and a single spring antenna topped with a star bob.
- **Gimmick:** a **giant wind-up key / crank on its back.** Hikaru's Kachi Dial twist "winds" it: every crank click fills a segment of the Kira Gauge (a real ratchet on the toy).
- **Weapon:** the **Tin Hammer Fists**: spring-loaded fists that clang, plus a chest hatch "Spark Plug" cannon.
- **Evolution:** *Base* is clean tin in red and cream. *Kira Overdrive* has the key spinning on its own and blazing; the lithograph stars light up and steam shoots from its shoulders. *TRI-GIGANT core* has the key moving to the combined robot's back as a giant crank that all three kids turn together.
- **Colors:** Tin Red `#D7282F`, Cream Enamel `#F5E6C8`, Brass Key `#E0A526`, Litho Blue `#2E6BD6`, Glow Yellow `#FFF04D`.
- **Silhouette:** a box robot with a huge key sticking out of its back: clear even as a shadow.
- **Why it's unique:** it's the *hero* answer to the Rust Legion's rusty tin grunts (same "family", but polished, bright and loved). That gives a story mirror, and the tactile crank is a strong toy hook. **Differentiation note:** the grunts must be purple and rust with no key; NOVA stays glossy, cream and red.

## 04 · HOMURA RIBBON — *"A scarf of living fire."*

- **Body:** a lean martial-arts hero with a sleeveless gi-style chest plate, wrapped forearms and wide stance legs. Not a ninja: this is a festival-kid kung-fu look.
- **Head:** a round helmet with a **half-mask covering the lower face** and a **single horizontal glowing slit** eye band. A short flame-exhaust "topknot" on the crown puffs fire when it's excited.
- **Gimmick:** the **flame ribbon scarf** is a set of clear orange-to-yellow gradient plastic ribbon parts that swap in: *scarf*, *whip*, *fist wraps* and *wings*. The ribbon "lives": it curls on its own to show emotion in the anime.
- **Weapon:** the **Homura Lash** (the ribbon whip) and the **Ribbon Drill Kick**.
- **Evolution:** *Base* has a short ribbon scarf. *Kira Overdrive* has the ribbon grow to three times its length and form a pair of fire wings with a star knot. *TRI-GIGANT core* has the ribbon wrap all three toys like a binding sash; it's the visual "glue" of the combination.
- **Colors:** Ember Red `#D9232E`, Charcoal `#2A2A33`, Flame Orange `#FF6B1A`, Flame Yellow `#FFD42A`, Ivory `#FFF6E5`.
- **Silhouette:** a small fighter with a huge swirling ribbon of fire around it.
- **Why it's unique:** it keeps the original's beloved scarf but turns it into *the* gimmick. The one-slit eye and half-mask read as a martial-arts hero, not a Gundam. Swappable clear parts make the toy easy to show off.

## 06 · PULLBACK NOVA — *"Pull back. Let go. BLAZE."*

- **Body:** an SD robot that folds into a **chunky pull-back toy racer** (a stubby Choro-Q-style mini car, not a realistic vehicle). Big rubber wheels sit on its calves and shoulders, and the car nose is its chest.
- **Head:** a **racing helmet** with a big wraparound tinted visor printed with the number "01" and a star. The eyes are just two dots of light behind the tint. A little rear spoiler sits on the back of the helmet.
- **Gimmick:** a **real pull-back motor**: drag it backwards and let go. In the game, holding the dash button "pulls back" and charges a zooming ram.
- **Weapon:** the **Nitro Knuckle** (a boost punch from its wheel-fists) and the **Burnout Ring**, a flaming tire track it lays down.
- **Evolution:** *Base* is a street racer. *Kira Overdrive* has flame decals that ignite for real, the spoiler becoming wings, and the wheels turning into star rims. *TRI-GIGANT core* is its car mode becoming the combined robot's legs/base and "engine"; the combination "drives" in.
- **Colors:** Racing Red `#E4202A`, Checker White `#FFFFFF`, Checker Black `#1A1A1A`, Visor Amber `#FFB02E`, Rim Chrome `#C9D3DD`.
- **Silhouette:** a chubby racer-kid with oversized wheels on its shoulders and calves.
- **Why it's unique:** its transformation is based on a *kids' toy car* (a pull-back mini racer), not on real vehicles like Transformers. The racing-helmet face is instantly readable, and the pull-back motor is a real physical play feature.

## 07 · GACHAMARU — *"A walking gacha machine."*

- **Body:** a small robot shaped like a **tabletop gacha machine**: a boxy red base body with a coin slot and a prize door, short legs, and arms that end in capsule-shaped mitts.
- **Head:** a **clear dome filled with tiny multicolour capsules**. Big friendly cartoon eyes are painted on the red collar under the dome, and a coin-slot mouth sits below them.
- **Gimmick:** a **real crank handle on its chest** (the Kachi Dial echo). Turning it pops out a random capsule from the prize door. In-game, each capsule is a random support buff or ammo type (a gacha move!).
- **Weapon:** the **Capsule Gatling**: its dome spins and rapid-fires mini capsules. The **Jackpot Shot** is a golden capsule that fires on a lucky crank.
- **Evolution:** *Base* is a red machine with mixed capsules. *Kira Overdrive* has every capsule in the dome turn gold, the machine lights spinning, and "JACKPOT!" text. *TRI-GIGANT core* has the machine becoming the combined robot's torso, with the other two toys "coming out of" its prize door as limbs.

- **Silhouette:** a gumball-machine robot: a round dome on a box.
- **Why it's unique:** it is literally the game's core loop turned into a character, and extremely ownable. Randomness as a combat mechanic fits the "gacha" theme perfectly. **Risk:** it reads as more comedic than heroic, so give it heroic brows and a stance.

## 08 · KIRA LANTERN — *"Armor on the outside, a star on the inside."*

- **Head:** a **coin face**: the head is a thick round coin, edge-on to the neck, with the star imprint forming the face (eyes set in the star's two upper points). A ridged coin edge runs around it like a helmet rim.
- **Gimmick:** **Cast-off.** The lantern armor pops off in pieces (spring-loaded on the toy) to show the fast, blazing inner frame. The armor pieces stay on the field as shields and turrets.
- **Weapon:** the **Lantern Shield** (armor mode) and the **Coin Blade**: a spinning coin-edge disc saw from its wrist in frame mode.
- **Evolution:** *Base* is armored, slow and tanky. *Kira Overdrive* is the cast-off frame: glowing gold, fast, with afterimages. *TRI-GIGANT core* is the frame acting as the "skeleton" the other toys armor onto: this mirrors its own cast-off gimmick.
- **Colors:** Lantern Red `#D8262C`, Frame White `#F7F7F2`, Coin Gold `#FFC41F`, Window Clear `#E6F7FF`, Glow Amber `#FF9A1F`.
- **Silhouette:** a round lantern-bellied robot with a coin for a head; after cast-off, a skinny star-sprinter.
- **Why it's unique:** the clear chest makes the Kira Spark *visible* (a story device you can see). Two forms in one toy, and a coin head nobody else has.
