# GACHARGE! Kachibot Roster: Heroic / Awesome / Fun / Weird-Cute

This is a design pass on 30 Kachibots in 10 capsule series. Edgy and villain toys (the Rust Legion) are covered in a separate doc.

## Ground rules this roster follows

- **No Gundam faces.** Nothing has a V-fin, a twin-eye "hero mask" or a faceplate with a mouth grille. Every face type is picked on purpose: a face on the torso, a mono-eye, a porthole, a headlight grille, an object-as-face, an animal or a mask.
- **Stay clear of other franchises:**
  - Transformers: no humanoid with car panels on it. The vehicle toys *are* the vehicle, standing up.
  - Medabots: no medal core.
  - Pokémon / Digimon: animals are always *objects first* (a stapler that is a croc, not a croc holding a stapler).
  - Bakugan: nothing springs open from a ball.
- **Object first, robot second.** A kid should name the everyday thing in the silhouette before they notice it's a robot. That joke is what the toy line runs on.
- **One gimmick = one visual hook = one moveset.** The toy's special move is its gimmick.
- **Shape language:** circle = friendly or support, square = tank, triangle = fast or duelist. Every entry is checked against the 64-px black-silhouette test.
- **Rarity is shown by material, not by new geometry.** The secret rare (★SR) in each series gets a finish callout: clear, chrome, pearl, glow or gold.

**Size and cost key**

| Class | Height (in-world) | Cost range | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | 4–6 cm; swarm units come 3–5 per capsule | 1–3 | Cheap, fast; often multiple bodies |
| M | ~10 cm (standard capsule toy) | 4–6 | The backbone of a squad |
| L | 13–16 cm (comes as a "jumbo capsule") | 6–8 | Slow, heavy hitters |
| XL | 20 cm+ (a boxed "Capsule Deluxe") | 9–10 | Lumbering heavies you build a squad around |

**Fusion key**

- **Twin-Twist** fuses 2 toys into one hybrid. Each entry lists a named partner and the result.
- **TRI-GIGANT** stacks 3 toys. Every toy has a preferred slot:
  - **TOP**: head, wings, cannons
  - **CORE**: torso and the main body
  - **BASE**: legs and locomotion

  Any three toys can fill the three slots. Each series has one *signature* set that unlocks a named giant with a unique super.

## SERIES 1: DAGASHI SNACK SERIES (駄菓子シリーズ)

*Penny-candy shop robots.*

- **Series trait:** a printed wrapper-label sash and a small "10円" price sticker decal somewhere on the body.
- **Capsule color:** candy pink.

### 01 RAMUNE ROCKET "BII-DAMA" (ラムネロケット・ビーダマ)

**Series** Dagashi Snack · **Size** M · **Cost** 4 · **Role** Shooter

**Silhouette hook:** a codd-neck ramune bottle on stubby legs, with a glass marble rattling in its pinched neck as its only eye.

**Visual design**
- The body is the classic hourglass ramune bottle in clear aqua resin, with fizz bubbles molded inside. The pinch doubles as its waist.
- It has no head. The marble in the neck is a mono-eye that rolls around to "look".
- A blue cap sits on top like a beret. The arms are the little push-plunger openers (*tamaoshi*).
- A wave-and-star paper label wraps the chest as a sash, with a 10円 sticker on the back.
- Chunky rubber feet, shaped like bottle-crate corners.

**Palette** `#7FD8E8` glass aqua · `#2F6FD6` cap blue · `#FF5A5F` label red · `#FFFFFF` foam · `#E8F9FF` glint

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Marble Shot:** spits its own eye-marble. The marble ricochets once, then rolls home. There's a short, funny "blind" reload while it's gone.
- **Melee, Cap Pop:** a plunger punch that bursts into foam.
- **Special, Shake-Shake Fizz:** hold to shake; the longer you shake, the bigger the jet. Aim it down for a rocket dash, or forward for a hose spray that shoves enemies.
- **Super, SODA GEYSER:** flips upside down, blasts straight up like a bottle rocket, then slams down in a splash dome that knocks everything back.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Konpeito Crew makes **Candy Cannon BII-DAMA**: it fires star-candy shrapnel marbles.
- **TRI-GIGANT:** TOP slot (a shoulder soda cannon).

**Why it's unique:** a shooter whose ammo is its own eye, and whose mobility is shaking a soda bottle.

### 02 KONPEITO CREW (こんぺいとうクルー)

**Series** Dagashi Snack · **Size** S (×5 per capsule) · **Cost** 1 · **Role** Swarm trickster

**Silhouette hook:** five spiky sugar-star gumdrops, each smaller than a thumb, bouncing like popcorn.

**Visual design**
- Each unit is a lumpy, bumpy konpeito sphere in a different pastel. Two dot eyes and a tiny mouth sit between the bumps.
- They have no limbs. They move by hopping and spinning.
- The leader (white) wears a tiny paper-cone hat made from the candy bag.
- Frosted, sugary translucent plastic. At rest they huddle in a little glass-jar-shaped base.

**Palette** `#FFB3C7` pink · `#FFF2A8` lemon · `#B8F0C8` mint · `#C9B8FF` lilac · `#FFFFFF` leader white

**Moveset** (you control the leader; the other four follow in formation)
- **Main shot, Sugar Flick:** each unit flicks a sugar crystal. That's low damage but five streams at once.
- **Melee, Pinball Pile-On:** all five bounce off the target in sequence and get a pinball combo counter.
- **Special, Star Tower:** the five stack into a wobbly totem to climb ledges or block a doorway. Enemies can knock it over.
- **Super, KONPEITO METEOR SHOWER:** they all launch skyward and rain down as glowing shooting stars.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Ramune Rocket (see 01). **Twin-Twist** with Mochitsuki Gekkō makes **Star-Mochi Gekkō**: the rabbit's mallet flings konpeito.
- **TRI-GIGANT:** can't fill a slot alone. The Crew clips on as decorative "star rivets" and gives any giant +1 armor.

**Why it's unique:** a 1-cost team of five that's cute enough to keep in a squad forever; it's the Pikmin feeling in candy form.

### 03 ★SR WATAAME TAISHŌ (わたあめ大将) "Cotton-Candy General"

**Series** Dagashi Snack · **Size** L · **Cost** 7 · **Role** Tank

**Silhouette hook:** a giant fluffy pink cloud on a single wooden stick, pogoing around like a pogo-stick general.

**Visual design**
- The body is a huge cotton-candy puff. Its face is a smug pair of eyes and a big mustache poking out of the fluff.
- A single disposable-chopstick "leg" gives it pogo locomotion, with a bouncy spring at the tip.
- A paper festival bag is worn as a samurai *jinbaori* cape, printed with a cartoon hero face.
- Two tiny wooden-stick arms hold a candy-floss *gunbai* (war fan).
- **SR finish:** glitter-flocked "fuzzy" paint with a pearl-rainbow sheen.

**Palette** `#FF9EC7` floss pink · `#B9E6FF` floss blue · `#E8C99A` wood stick · `#FFF7FB` sugar white · `#FF5A8A` cheek

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Floss Puff:** lobbed sticky candy clumps that slow whatever they hit.
- **Melee, Pogo Stomp:** a bouncing stomp. Chain three stomps for a bigger shockwave.
- **Special, Sugar Sponge:** projectiles that hit it get *absorbed* and make it visibly bigger. Size means more HP but also a bigger hitbox and slower movement.
- **Super, SUGAR STORM:** spins on its stick like a cotton-candy machine and throws a sticky pink tornado that glues enemies in place.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Awadate Twister makes **Meringue Shōgun**: the whisk spins the floss into armor.
- **TRI-GIGANT:** CORE slot (a giant fluffy chest that works as a damage sponge).

**Why it's unique:** a tank that grows when you shoot it, so the enemy has to *choose* whether to feed it.

## SERIES 2: STATIONERY SERIES (文房具シリーズ)

*School-desk samurai.*

- **Series trait:** a metallic silver "blade" edge somewhere on each toy, plus a school-name-sticker nameplate.
- **Capsule color:** notebook blue with ruled lines.

### 04 BUNMAWASHI MUSASHI (ぶんまわし武蔵) "Compass Samurai"

**Series** Stationery · **Size** M · **Cost** 5 · **Role** Brawler (duelist)

**Silhouette hook:** a drafting compass standing as a samurai: one needle leg, one pencil leg, and a hinge-knob topknot.

**Visual design**
- The body *is* the compass. The two legs form an A-frame stance, the hinge is the waist, and the knurled handle knob is a topknot (*chonmage*).
- The face is a small visor-slit in the hinge plate. Two painted eyebrows give it a stern look.
- The pencil-leg is a stubby yellow pencil with a sharpened graphite tip. The needle leg is chrome.
- A protractor is worn as a crescent back-banner (*sashimono*) with degree marks.
- A ruler katana is sheathed at the hip.

**Palette** `#C9CED6` steel · `#FFD23F` pencil yellow · `#2B2D42` graphite · `#E63946` sash red · `#F8F8F2` protractor clear

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Pencil Dart:** flicks graphite lead points in a fan.
- **Melee, Ruler Iaido:** a quick-draw ruler slash with a long reach.
- **Special, Perfect Circle:** plants the needle leg and spins the pencil leg around it, drawing a glowing circle on the floor. Enemies inside take ticking damage. The circle also blocks projectiles along its rim.
- **Super, NITŌRYŪ: DOUBLE CIRCLE:** draws two interlocking circles (a Venn-diagram duel field). Anything caught where they overlap takes triple hits.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Hotchkiss Gator makes **Bunbōgu Shōgun**: a compass-sword with stapler gauntlets.
- **TRI-GIGANT:** BASE slot (the A-frame stance becomes the giant's legs).

**Why it's unique:** its zone control is geometry. You literally draw the arena you want to fight in.

### 05 HOTCHKISS GATOR (ホッチキス・ゲーター) "Stapler Croc"

**Series** Stationery · **Size** M · **Cost** 4 · **Role** Brawler / trickster

**Silhouette hook:** a desk stapler whose hinged top is a crocodile jaw, trotting low on four eraser-block feet.

**Visual design**
- A long rectangular stapler body with the top arm shaped as a croc snout. Small bumps along the top read as croc scales.
- The eyes are two round pushpin heads, one red and one blue, set on top of the snout.
- The staple strip shows as a row of silver "teeth" through a clear window on its side.
- Four pink eraser-block feet and a tail made from a clipped paper-clip chain.
- The staple remover is worn as a tiny chin-guard fang.

**Palette** `#2EC4B6` stapler teal · `#0B3954` deep navy · `#E0E0E0` staple silver · `#FF9FB2` eraser pink · `#FFBF00` pushpin yellow

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Staple Spit:** rapid-fire staples at short-to-mid range.
- **Melee, Chomp-Chak:** a jaw-slam bite. If it lands, the target is pinned for 1 second.
- **Special, Pin Down:** fires a big staple arc into the floor. Any enemy standing there is stapled in place and has to mash to escape.
- **Super, DEATH ROLL BINDER:** grabs a foe and rolls, stapling them into a paper bundle. The bundle is thrown as a projectile.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Musashi (see 04).
- **TRI-GIGANT:** TOP slot (a croc-jaw head). A giant with a stapler jaw can bite other giants.

**Why it's unique:** the stun-lock brawler is an office supply everyone has, and the gimmick is the stapler's own sound: *ka-CHAK!*

### 06 ★SR FUDEBAKO FORTRESS GX (筆箱要塞GX) "Pencil-Case Fortress"

**Series** Stationery · **Size** XL · **Cost** 9 · **Role** Tank · **Transforms**

**Silhouette hook:** the 1980s two-sided magnetic Japanese pencil case with 10 pop-out buttons. It unfolds into a walking castle that has *gadgets on every face*.

**Visual design**
- **Case mode:** a closed, flat rectangular box with a robot-anime sticker on the lid. It skates around on a hidden sharpener wheel.
- **Fortress mode:** stands up on end. The lid swings open into a shoulder-mounted rampart. The two tiers become the torso and the pelvis.
- It's covered in pop-out buttons. Each one pops a gadget: a sharpener turret, a thermometer antenna, a magnifier eye, a built-in clock and a pencil-tray drawbridge.
- The face is a magnifying-glass mono-eye in the center of the lid's inner wall.
- **SR finish:** holographic prism lid sticker and chrome buttons.

**Palette** `#3A86FF` case blue · `#FFBE0B` button yellow · `#FB5607` sticker orange · `#8338EC` hologram violet · `#F1F1F1` inner white

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Sharpener Gatling:** a pop-up turret sprays wood shavings.
- **Melee, Lid Slam:** swings the magnetic lid like a castle gate. It hits hard with big knockback.
- **Special, Button Barrage:** press the special to "pop" a random button from 6 gadgets:
  - a shield wall
  - a heal-clock (rewinds 3 seconds of HP)
  - a magnifier sniper beam
  - a drawbridge ramp that allies can climb
  - a compass radar that reveals stealthed enemies
  - an eraser mine
- **Super, TEN-BUTTON FULL OPEN:** pops *every* gadget at once in a spectacular fireworks moment.
- **Transform:** case mode drives fast and small, and is immune to knockback. Fortress mode is slow and massive.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with any S-size toy: the S toy rides *inside* the pencil tray as a turret gunner.
- **TRI-GIGANT:** CORE slot (the classic combiner chest).

**Why it's unique:** a nostalgia object that's *already* a gadget toy. Its moveset is a gacha machine inside a gacha toy.

## SERIES 3: KITCHEN GADGET SERIES (台所ガジェットシリーズ)

*The kitchen appliances wake up at midnight.*

- **Series trait:** a round power-lamp "cheek" on each toy that turns from green (ready) to red (cooking).
- **Capsule color:** mint enamel.

### 07 SUIHAN-OH (炊飯王) "Rice-Cooker King"

**Series** Kitchen Gadget · **Size** L · **Cost** 7 · **Role** Tank / support

**Silhouette hook:** a round, squat 1990s rice cooker on stubby legs, with a flip-up lid it uses as a shield and a rice paddle as a scepter.

**Visual design**
- A round, egg-shaped white body with a floral print band. The body is a face-torso: the LCD panel *is* its face, showing pixel eyes that change expression.
- The domed lid flips up as a huge round shield, and steam vents out the top like a crest.
- One arm holds a *shamoji* (rice paddle) scepter. The other is a cord-reel arm that extends like a whip.
- Four tiny rubber-foot legs, and a "keep warm" lamp on the chest.

**Palette** `#FAFAF5` enamel white · `#8BC34A` floral green · `#FF7043` lamp orange · `#FFD166` rice gold · `#37474F` LCD dark

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Onigiri Lob:** throws rice balls. They damage enemies, and allies can pick them up as heals.
- **Melee, Shamoji Swat:** a paddle bash with a small stun.
- **Special, Lid Guard:** flips up the lid shield. It blocks everything from the front, and blocked hits charge the "Keep Warm" meter.
- **Super, TAKIAGARI! (Done Cooking!):** plays the jingle, erupts a steam pillar that heals every ally in range, and knocks enemies back.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Toast-Ace makes **Breakfast Carrier**: toast drones launch from the rice pot.
- **TRI-GIGANT:** CORE. Signature set **GOCHISŌ DAI-OH** (ごちそう大王) = Toast-Ace (TOP) + Suihan-Oh (CORE) + Awadate Twister (BASE, a spinning whisk drill-leg). Super: **FULL-COURSE FINISHER**, which serves the enemy a very large, very hot meal.

**Why it's unique:** a tank that talks through a pixel LCD and heals with rice balls. It's grandma's-house energy as a fortress.

### 08 AWADATE TWISTER (泡立てツイスター) "Whisk Dervish"

**Series** Kitchen Gadget · **Size** S · **Cost** 2 · **Role** Trickster

**Silhouette hook:** a balloon whisk flipped upside down: a spinning wire cage on top of a tiny handle-body.

**Visual design**
- The head is the wire whisk loop. Inside the cage bounces a tiny ball eye (like a whisk-ball whisk).
- The body is the whisk handle, with a hanging-loop "antenna" at the base that curls like a tail.
- Two little spatula arms and a round suction-cup foot, so it moves like a spinning top.
- A splash of meringue is molded on the top of the wires.

**Palette** `#D9DEE3` wire silver · `#FF6F91` handle pink · `#FFF6E0` meringue cream · `#6C5CE7` accent violet

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Froth Flick:** flicks foam blobs that blur the target's vision (a screen splat).
- **Melee, Whirl:** a spinning top-dash that juggles small enemies.
- **Special, Meringue Floor:** whips a foam puddle that slows everyone except itself.
- **Super, STIFF PEAKS:** whips the foam so hard it sets into a maze of meringue walls around the enemy.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Wataame (see 03).
- **TRI-GIGANT:** BASE (a spinning drill-leg).

**Why it's unique:** a tiny chaos gremlin that can wall off a giant enemy with dessert.

### 09 ★SR POP-UP TWIN JET "TOAST-ACE" (トーストエース)

**Series** Kitchen Gadget · **Size** M · **Cost** 6 · **Role** Flyer / summoner · **Transforms**

**Silhouette hook:** a chrome retro pop-up toaster whose two bread slots are jet intakes. The two toast slices launch as wingmen.

**Visual design**
- **Walker mode:** a toaster with stubby legs. The face is its chrome side, which reflects a cartoon face drawn on in a fingerprint smudge. The lever on the side is a cocking arm.
- **Jet mode:** tips forward. The cord becomes a tail streamer, and fold-out butter-knife wings fan from the sides.
- Two bread-slice drones have jam-smear visors and crust armor. They ride in the slots until they're launched.
- **SR finish:** full mirror-chrome with a "golden-brown" gradient on the toasts.

**Palette** `#DDE3EA` chrome · `#FFB347` golden toast · `#C1440E` crust · `#E63946` jam red · `#2D3142` dial black

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Crumb Cannon:** a stream of hot crumbs from the slots.
- **Melee, Lever Uppercut:** slams the lever down and pops upward.
- **Special, POP!:** launches the two toast drones. They orbit and shoot until their timer ends, then fly back and "re-toast" (cooldown). Holding the button sets the browning dial: a longer charge makes stronger but slower-returning drones.
- **Super, BURNT-TOAST BOMBING RUN:** transforms to jet mode, flies a strafing run, and the drones dive-bomb in flames.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Suihan-Oh (see 07).
- **TRI-GIGANT:** TOP (the wings and jet pack).

**Why it's unique:** a summoner whose minions are breakfast. Every pull gets the *pop* sound effect.

## SERIES 4: STREET-RACER SERIES (ストリートレーサーシリーズ)

*Pullback-car heroes.*

- **Series trait:** a checkered-flag stripe and a race number, all rolling on chunky rubber tires.
- **Capsule color:** racing orange.
- **Design rule:** these are *never* humanoids made of car panels. The car stands up, and that's the whole joke.

### 10 PULLBACK HAYATE (プルバック疾風)

**Series** Street-Racer · **Size** M · **Cost** 4 · **Role** Brawler · **Transforms**

**Silhouette hook:** a squat, egg-shaped pullback car doing a permanent wheelie, fists out, like a kid's toy car stood up on its back bumper.

**Visual design**
- A super-deformed, chunky car. In robot mode it stands on its rear wheels, and the front wheels become boxing-glove fists.
- The face is on the grille: headlight eyes and a license-plate mouth that reads "HYT-01".
- A huge rear spoiler works as a shoulder cape. A wind-up spring motor is visible through a clear roof.
- A race number "7" and flames stickered on the doors.
- **Transform:** it just falls forward onto four wheels. It takes one frame and is very satisfying.

**Palette** `#FF3B30` racing red · `#FFFFFF` stripe white · `#111111` tire black · `#FFD60A` headlight yellow · `#00A6FB` window blue

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Exhaust Pop:** short-range backfire puffs.
- **Melee, Wheel-Fist Rush:** a spinning tire combo that leaves skid marks.
- **Special, Pull Back:** hold to back up. The distance you back up equals the charge. Release to go car mode and rocket forward in a ramming dash. Hitting a wall stuns *you*.
- **Super, TOP-GEAR COMET:** a max-charge ram that loops around the arena twice and leaves a fire trail.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Demae Soba-Kaze makes **Delivery Dragster**: the scooter becomes a side-car gun.
- **TRI-GIGANT:** BASE (wheel legs). Signature set **KAIDŌ-GIGANT** (街道ギガント) = Soba-Kaze (TOP) + Hayate (CORE) + Dekotora Daimyō (BASE, a truck undercarriage). Super: **SENBON-ZAKURA HIGHWAY**, a light-parade charge.

**Why it's unique:** the charge mechanic is the physical toy's pullback. The kid watching can mime it.

### 11 DEMAE "SOBA-KAZE" (出前そば風) "Delivery Scooter"

**Series** Street-Racer · **Size** S · **Cost** 3 · **Role** Support · **Carries a rider**

**Silhouette hook:** a little moped with a tower of steaming soba bowls on a spring-arm tray. That tower is taller than the scooter.

**Visual design**
- A cute step-through scooter whose round headlight is its single big eye. The handlebars are antennae-arms.
- On the back, the classic *demae-ki* suspension arm holds a stack of lacquered bowls that wobble with every bump.
- A shop *noren* flag reads "そば".
- An empty rider seat: any S-size toy can sit there.

**Palette** `#F4F1DE` cream body · `#C0392B` lacquer red · `#1B1B1B` bowl black · `#6AB187` noren green · `#F2C14E` headlight

**Moveset**
- **Main shot, Chopstick Flick:** throws chopsticks as darts.
- **Melee, Kickstand Kick.**
- **Special, Order Up!:** zips to an ally and delivers a bowl: heal plus a speed buff. If your aim is bad, a bowl dropped on an enemy scalds them.
- **Rider:** an S-size toy rides the back seat. The rider shoots while Soba-Kaze drives, for example Konpeito, Piyo-Piyo or Ryūsei Kid.
- **Super, RUSH-HOUR RELAY:** laps the arena, dropping bowls to every ally at once.

**Fusion**
- **Twin-Twist** with Hayate (see 10).
- **TRI-GIGANT:** TOP (the bowl tower becomes a hat-cannon).

**Why it's unique:** a healer who's a delivery guy, and the mobile taxi for your swarm toys.

### 12 ★SR DEKOTORA DAIMYŌ (デコトラ大名) "Art-Truck Lord"

**Series** Street-Racer · **Size** XL · **Cost** 9 · **Role** Tank / transport · **Carries riders**

**Silhouette hook:** a Japanese *dekotora* art truck, dripping with chrome and lights. It rears up so the cab becomes a lordly head and chest and the cargo box becomes a mobile castle keep.

**Visual design**
