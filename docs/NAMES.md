# Name Brainstorm — title chosen: **GACHARGE!** (ガチャージ)

The ideas below came from three brainstorm angles: punchy anime/arcade titles, gacha/Japanese wordplay, and
in-world terms. The conflict checks were quick web searches only. Run a real trademark search (USPTO /
J-PlatPat / EUIPO) before locking anything in.

## Title shortlist

| Title | Katakana | Pitch | Quick conflict check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GACHARGE!** | ガチャージ | Gacha + charge: crank the machine and your toy charges into battle | No match found |
| **TWISTBOTS** | ツイストボッツ | Twist the knob, a bot pops out; works as a toy-line word like Beyblade | Mostly clear (a small GitHub org) |
| **CAPSULION** | カプセリオン | Sounds like an '80s super-robot show; could also name the combined mega-form | No match (Capsela, an old construction toy, is only similar) |
| **CAPSULE KAISER** | カプセルカイザー | Alliterative mecha swagger; "Kaiser" hints at fusion | Clear |
| **KACHIBOTS** | カチボッツ | Kachi-kachi is the crank click, and *kachi* (勝ち) means "win" | Not checked as a title yet |
| **GASHA GATTAI** | ガシャガッタイ | Covers both core mechanics (pull + combine); better as a subtitle | Mostly clear ("Gashapon" is a Bandai trademark) |
| **CAPSULE KNIGHTS** | カプセルナイツ | Heroic; subtitle *Rise of the Scrap King* | Close to Bandai's "Capsule Guardian" toy line |

**Avoid:** Toybox Titans (an existing Steam game with the same premise), GachaGear (the Steam game *Gatcha Gear*), GachaMecha
(*Mecha Gacha* and *Mecha Kucha Gacha Defence*), Gasha Guardians (Bandai's Capsule Guardian), Capsule Corner (Dragon Ball's
Capsule Corp.).

A near-competitor worth knowing about: *GASHAPWN!* on itch.io, a 1v1 capsule-toy battler in GameCube style.

## In-world terms (recommended ← alternatives)

- **Living toys:** **Kachibots** ← PopGears, Kapsuloids, Gachanauts
- **Hero team:** **Starcap Squad** (logo: a star on a capsule lid) ← Team Kirameki, Brave Capsule, Crank Crew
- **Villain:** **Emperor Junkrown**, a throne-body made of broken toys; "Gachaos" could be his final form ← Kaiser Kludge,
  Lord Scraptor, King Wreckoning
- **Villain army:** the **Rust Legion**, with grunts called **Rustlings** (purified after defeat, then they join you)
- **What brings toys to life:** the **Kira Spark**, from a falling-star coin dropped into a gacha machine
- **Wrist device:** the **Kachi Dial**, twisted like a gacha handle to summon
- **Catchphrase:** "Twist it! Kachi-Kachi, KIRA ON!"
- **Partner mech:** **NOVABLAZE** (shouted as "NOVA!") ← BLAZE, KIRAMARU, STARBOLT
- **Rival mech:** **AOGIRI** (blue mist; fire vs. mist)
- **Hometown / shop:** **Tsumiki Town** (*tsumiki* = toy blocks) / **Kororin Dagashi** (the sound of a rolling capsule)
